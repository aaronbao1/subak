## Scope rules

- **In scope:** implement supported mechanism guards under declared data support, preserving any identification limit.
- **Conditional:** supplied finite parameter/state/contract assumptions can define a useful executable scenario without enterprise calibration. Do not call sensitivity weights fitted probabilities or causal effects. Every conditional record separates a testable guard from its unresolved remainder.
- **Out of scope:** exclude the external or unidentifiable subclaim; preserve its required in-scope semantic/authority guard. These records are retained, not discarded.
- **Independent outputs:** missing tariffs can disable money while usage remains available; absent roster can disable structural fitting while aggregate forecasting remains available; failed forecasts preserve measured actuals and manual reviewed finance work.
- **Verification:** record exact code symbol/test function/asserted scope, executed command/result, hashes and residual limitation. A generic passing suite is not evidence for every conjunct.

Automated donor recommendations, chosen-policy replay and a complete structured proposal-impact summary remain conditional and unimplemented. Current defenses are manual finance review, protected-budget rules, actuals visibility and no safe-transfer/optimality claim. Risk arithmetic alone cannot close donor-selection cases.
