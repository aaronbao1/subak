## One mechanism, several defenses

Add one immutable control history and one pure event reducer to the joint scenario path, rather than independent cap, queue, retry and refund modules. Suggested implementation targets are `src/forecasting/execution.py` plus joint-path scenario wiring; these paths are proposed. Each event carries tenant/pool/path identity, stable event and physical-attempt IDs, occurrence time, available time, deterministic tie order, policy revision, root/continuation role and event kind. Workflow owns genealogy and transitions; controls owns feasibility and conserved state; economics owns assumptions governing behavioral response.

For one control scope and unit, maintain `(period, limit, settled_debit, reservations_by_attempt, running_attempts, queue, pending_liability, enforcement_mode)`. Rate/token quotas have separate units and reset rules; a money cap cannot be compared directly to tokens. Policies explicitly distinguish advisory, hard reserved-bound and estimated/postpaid modes. Multiple team/org scopes are checked atomically against the same proposed event; failure leaves every scope unchanged. Internal allocation changes never alter any control scope unless a separate externally controlled scenario explicitly says so.

A proposal can queue, deny or reserve/start. Queued work has not consumed execution and need not hold money unless the policy says it reserves on enqueue. On actual start, acquire concurrency and reserve required liability. `settle` removes the matching reservation and records actual charge exactly once; negative signed corrections need a linked settlement revision rather than another completion. Release execution concurrency on acknowledged end, independently from financial finality. A timeout or cancellation request cannot erase potential provider charge. An acknowledged cancellation can stop future events while retaining already incurred or uncertain settlement. Carry opening active attempts and reservations at origin; never respawn or reserve them as new roots.

The spend identity is `available = limit - settled_debit - sum(reservations)` for the declared control basis, not an invoice or cash identity. Under estimated enforcement, actual settlement may exceed reservation and available may become negative. Preserve that overshoot; do not clamp it away. A hard-cap claim requires verified upper-bounded billable marks with actual charge no greater than reservation and every physical start participating in the same atomic ledger. Unknown rates, delayed provider activity and advisory budgets cannot provide that guarantee.

## Root modes and observational meaning

Restart `architecture.md:104–117` is decisive. In offered-demand mode, roots represent intended AI work and pass admission once. Preserve queued/denied roots as unmet or deferred demand with reason and scope, not evidence of low adoption. Actual first execution establishes delivered starts and first trial. In delivered-start mode, sampled roots already represent throughput conditional on historical controls: bypass root admission. Validate path feasibility where enough control evidence exists; do not quietly reject a second fraction of starts. When the delivered law is unbounded or opening liabilities are unknown, withhold hard-cap assurance while retaining useful conditional mean forecasts. Continuations keep their own explicit event semantics; bypassing root admission does not create unrestricted retries.

A policy change scenario also needs an identification label. Removing a cap from a delivered-start model cannot recover suppressed demand. Use explicit offered-demand assumptions or observed proposals/denials; do not multiply a delivered forecast by an invented rebound factor. A logging gap differs from a cap denial, and both differ from an employee declining to use AI.

## Top implementation changes and checks

1. Define root mode, enforcement mode, control basis, known/effective policy times and opening outstanding attempts in the scenario contract. Pin these into its manifest.
2. Implement one pure chronological reducer with typed transitions, stable tie ordering, duplicate-event detection and all-scope atomic feasibility. Return accepted/queued/denied events, final state and unresolved assumptions alongside usage.
3. Integrate reservation and settlement with priced physical attempts, preserving billed failures, cancellation liability and queue carryover into the exact requested period.
4. Add support gates for hard-limit risk and changed-cap counterfactuals; preserve ordinary conditional forecast output when those stronger claims are unavailable.

Proposed executable fixtures, not yet run: two simultaneous team calls competing for one shared allowance; one call holding a reservation while another queues; late billed failure after cancellation; duplicate settlement replay; reset with a call spanning the boundary; stricter policy known only after origin; cap lowered below existing commitments; paired offered and delivered fixtures proving exactly one admission stage; advisory allocation change with identical execution; same state after input permutation under explicit tie order. Verify queue mass `opening + arrivals = starts + explicit abandonment + closing`, unique physical starts, reservation/settlement conservation and finite event limits. A finite simulation event cap must yield `truncated/incomplete`, never natural success or an estimated zero tail. Synthetic fixtures establish mechanism behavior only; they do not validate real provider enforcement or forecast accuracy.
