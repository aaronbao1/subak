## Current evidence and the smallest useful extension

`context.NumericAssumption` and `workload_scenario` already require a named supplied nonnegative Decimal multiplier, restrict adjustment to future reporting dates, and label the result conditional rather than causal. `pricing.price_period` already supports exact frozen-use arithmetic under explicit limited synthetic tariffs; `price_paths` prices each weighted usage path. `tests/test_pricing.py:test_price_only_scenario_freezes_usage_and_higher_usage_can_outweigh_cut` captures the key rebound identity: a move from 100 units at rate 10 to 100 at 8 reduces expense from 1000 to 800, but 150 units at 8 costs 1200. These tests support arithmetic and immutability; they do not validate demand response. `service.py` separately rejects internal allocation and fixed obligations as stochastic variable demand. Preserve these protections in the new kernel.

Recommend four changes, implemented through shared contracts rather than separate economic modules:

1. Every result and scenario declares its estimand: `current_policy`, `frozen_usage_repricing`, `same_task_migration`, or `behavior_response`. Include the task/opportunity population held fixed, count basis, policy/catalog revisions, response-parameter artifact and predictive/supplied/causally-identified status. A changed tokenizer or workflow is migration and requires new usage marks; it cannot silently reuse frozen physical units.
2. A single context-conditioned behavior transition receives eligible tasks, feasible routes, access/defaults, budget/control state, lagged experience, offered price and task-conditioned capability. It outputs adoption/task/route decisions before the existing workflow/marks/pricing stages. Fixed defaults can be deterministic; no softmax is compulsory. Missing local coefficients should permit explicitly supplied sensitivity paths and a simple unchanged-share baseline. Unknown empirical response is not grounds to discard the known measured baseline.
3. Keep chronological feedback acyclic: information available at origin generates decisions; delivered outcomes update later familiarity, budgets and beliefs. An internal allocation-only change leaves physical execution paths unchanged unless a named behavioral response is enabled. An external hard-cap scenario changes admission through the controls kernel. A baseline fitted to delivered roots must not receive another adoption/admission multiplier; its cap-removal counterfactual requires intended/denied records or explicit unmet-demand sensitivity.
4. Compare scenarios on paired background draws and preserve evidence limits. Sensitivity grids have no implicit probability. Conditional mixtures require declared weights and provenance, while empirical breach-probability qualification remains a separate gate. Driver explanations store the decomposition order or Shapley rule and interaction terms; components must sum to the exact scenario-minus-baseline delta.

## Hard cases that remain useful in scope

A stronger model may lower tokens on old tasks while increasing total spend through hard new tasks, broader adoption or longer autonomous workflows. Report fixed-task migration and whole-portfolio response separately, including manual/deferred/unknown-provider outside options. Lower tracked-source usage after provider migration is a valid covered-source forecast, not proof of company savings. Latency, review effort, failure risk, integration friction and defaults can outweigh token price for a user whose employer pays the bill. This supports feasible choices and supplied switching-lag scenarios; it does not justify inventing a universal utility coefficient.

Likewise, shared budget grants and rate changes are different interventions. Giving research another 50,000 internal credits changes the planning envelope, not its provider's tariff. The operative mechanism may be perceived quota, chargeback or management expectations. Record that mechanism rather than training a token-price elasticity on allocation changes. Choosing the team with maximum apparent headroom is another intervention: donor diagnostics must assess the selected population, and allocation conservation cannot be described as expense savings or optimal business value.

Novel compound cases in the edge ledger combine cheaper pricing with task expansion; cap suppression with delivered-only history; routing selection with task difficulty; forecasts with policy response; and relative model improvement with absolute migration to missing sources. The defenses reuse four invariants: explicit target/population, chronological state, conservation, and capability/evidence gates.

## What cannot be identified from the supplied input

Tracked executions do not identify all manual, abandoned or denied opportunities; observational route outcomes do not identify never-selected route success; historical provider prices do not identify a transportable causal elasticity; numerical forecasts alone do not establish realized savings or business utility. These limitations constrain causal claims and fitted parameters, not implementation of conditional scenarios. Use narrow valid predictions, identified components, supplied sensitivity ranges and unknown residuals. Do not assign zero to unobserved outside cost or equal probabilities to an arbitrary stress grid.
