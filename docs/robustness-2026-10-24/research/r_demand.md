## Small common kernel

The highest-value addition is one typed stochastic path generator with two explicit observation modes and an aggregate adapter. Do not add an edge-specific predictor for each scenario.

1. **Delivered-root mode:** fit a coverage-qualified daily hurdle count, `N = 0` with probability `1-pi`, otherwise `1 + NB(mu,k)`. Its mean is `pi*(1+mu)` and conditional variance is `pi*(mu+mu²/k)+pi*(1-pi)*(1+mu)²`. This is shifted-NB, not a zero-truncated NB with interchangeable formulas. A seeded gamma–Poisson sampler implements NB with a finite positive dispersion. Use proper support and finite parameters; negative values clipped from a scalar regression are not an identified count law.
2. **Opportunity mode:** model observed or explicitly assumed opportunities and intended AI/manual/deferred/abandoned channels, then let the single shared chronological execution kernel determine actual executed roots. Without representative opportunity/denial evidence, expose the delivered-root baseline and declared demand-sensitivity scenarios. Delivered logs do not identify unmet work. The root emitter must not increment adoption independently of actual execution.
3. **Common path and composition:** draw persistent organization/project state once per path, reuse it in root occurrence/intensity and downstream difficulty/token marks, and assign task types jointly so class counts sum to the emitted total. A surge may mean many simple jobs or a few expensive jobs; do not silently assume independent marks or one universal severity multiplier. Include scheduled time shifts once, with their source/status. Announced dates can be known covariates; uncertain execution dates are scenario draws. A launch postponed past month end changes the remainder target, not just its average level.
4. **Pooling and finite moments:** retain population uncertainty for new teams/people rather than zero-filled prehistory. Shrink toward comparable role/project populations with explicit fit IDs and no hidden inference-batch donors. Persistent heavy-user effects need room to differ from peers. Avoid Student-t effects on an unbounded log rate: their exponential moment is infinite. Finite normal-mixture effects with controlled scales give a tractable prior; empirical accuracy still requires evaluation. Forecasting internal cohorts does not require personal budgets or a personal interface.
5. **Queue and identity conservation:** separate new roots from active work at origin, deferred opportunities from newly created opportunities, and human roots from physical request descendants. Deferred tasks keep their identity when admitted later. Taxonomy revisions require a mapping or an explicit comparability break; renaming tasks should not create demand. Stable pseudonymous IDs suffice, without prompt bodies.

A full hierarchical fit is a later empirical improvement. An initial implementation can use pooled sufficient statistics, versioned scenario parameters and synthetic mechanism fixtures, labeled accordingly. It must keep direct aggregate models as real challengers whenever task telemetry does not exist. Aggregate spend can remain forecastable while task/adoption decompositions are unavailable.

## Verification that changes the answer

Required invariants are stronger than sampling a plausible histogram. Check seeded replay; nonnegative integer roots; exact task-class conservation; `pi=0` always zero; `pi=1,mu=0` always one; sample mean/variance against the formulas within reported Monte Carlo error; duplicate-root idempotence; and no root increase on retry/resume. Complete zeros are observations; missing coverage cannot enter their denominator. First executed root sets ever-used and occurs exactly once. Reduced delivered mode must demonstrably bypass second admission. Full mode must account for every opportunity as admitted, queued, denied, manual, deferred or abandoned under a mutually exclusive status convention.

Check common-factor behavior with a paired stress fixture: identical marginal count/severity inputs but positively coupled shared state should change cumulative team tails; independent daily jitter should not stand in for persistent deadline uncertainty. Hold a heavy user out and compare cold-start predictive variance with an established comparable user. Move a launch date across the requested half-open month boundary and verify the current-period sum changes without losing or duplicating deferred work. A pure taxonomy relabeling must preserve company totals.

For real-data evaluation, preserve current rolling as-of origins and all-opportunity denominators; compare the joint mechanism against existing direct aggregate baselines on exact cumulative tokens/cost. Report sparse/zero-history, heavy-concentration and deadline windows separately. Count fit alone cannot promote spend forecasts or budget risk. Synthetic fixtures verify identities and mechanism behavior; they cannot establish enterprise accuracy, rebound elasticity or the prevalence of these edges.
