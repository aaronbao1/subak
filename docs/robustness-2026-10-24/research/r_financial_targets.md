## Five changes that defend many cases

1. **Make components carry compatible identities, rather than bare amounts.** The narrow concrete defect is that a ready `ForecastResult` with `measure='metered_usage'`, `unit='tokens'`, `future=10` and `fixed=100` is accepted. I executed that constructor and observed `status='ready'`; `service.py:97–98` likewise parses a bare `fixed_obligations` Decimal independent of target type. No cash sum is displayed today, so this is a latent semantic-integrity gap rather than an observed double-counted dashboard total. The immediate fix is to reject fixed monetary obligations on physical usage targets. The extensible fix is an immutable component carrying role, target/basis fingerprint, unit/currency, interval, source/economic lineage, known time and coverage. A `late_incurred` component cannot be reused as future demand; a monetary fee cannot silently become tokens. Missing remains unknown.

2. **Add a small disjoint-composition contract before any all-in capability.** Use recorded variable, late incurred, future continuation, scheduled fixed and signed correction roles; check matching tenant/basis/period and nonoverlapping economic coverage. Included activity is an explanatory usage view, never a second subscription expense. A fixed schedule must say whether the charge is already in recorded data. Require a certificate of included/excluded categories before emitting an all-in value. Current `all_in_total=None` is the correct fallback, but an in-scope composition path with explicit fixtures is more useful than permanently calling every composite unsupported. A known fixed schedule is deterministic evidence; it need not wait for a forecasting model.

3. **Treat balances and flows as different semantic types.** `Target(...measure='metered_usage', unit='credits', categories=('balance',), support='nonnegative')` is accepted with `additive=True`; I executed this probe. A source balance can then be mislabeled as daily consumption by a normalized adapter. Credits are ambiguous even without a live vendor: included entitlement, prepaid asset balance, promotion, billing correction, refund and internal budget all answer different questions. Add a quantity role (`flow`, `stock`, `schedule`) or a verified adapter capability that prevents stock/budget series entering additive demand. Do not infer a dollar conversion from the word credits. Native internal allocation may stay a backend planning object rather than forcing it into monetary demand `Target`.

4. **Use one stateful contract path, then derive displayed deltas.** Shared included usage, commitment consumption, later corrections and volume tiers couple prefix and future. Future usage need not imply positive marginal cost: the existing 100-at-10 plus one-unit-at-volume-8 test produces a -192 repricing increment. Label that `repricing_delta`, retain nonnegative physical demand and avoid feeding it to an arrival/count model. Extending to simple synthetic obligation/entitlement state is feasible without implementing every vendor rule. Real seat transitions, promotional eligibility, renewal terms and credit expiry remain conditional until a verified adapter supplies those terms. The pricing worker owns tariff matching and origin/reset enforcement.

5. **Compare only like financial targets and preserve distinct outcomes.** Keep usage forecast, estimated valuation, reported charge, invoice liability, payment and internal allocation separate. The same service event can revise an old incurred period and a newer invoice, while cash remains unknown. Supported direct charge forecasting remains useful for opaque stable categories; usage-then-pricing is useful where meters and contracts reconcile. Neither should automatically overwrite the other. Budget redistribution changes assigned envelopes; it does not prove avoided vendor payment, release a commitment or purchase prepaid credits. Existing consumer guards already avoid claiming safe donor funds; scope worker owns that boundary.
