## Central invariant

**An origin may use only quantities available then, and an unavailable component may not acquire the value zero merely because another component or delivery is complete.** Preserve immutable physical-meter observations and financial charge observations as distinct views. Reconcile their scopes; never add them simply because their numerical units agree. Exact conservation applies to represented covered activity, not to unobserved enterprise activity. Completeness, finality, receipt success, roster exposure and numerical model eligibility are different facts.

This directly follows the internal discussion's fragmented Microsoft/Cursor receipts and projection-versus-actuals debate (internal transcript line 6, passages beginning “The data is also fragmented” and “you still need actuals”), its concern about ingestion and repeated live updates, mentor lines 18–25's September service/December bill example, and master-plan lines 27–43 and 97–109. ## Verified defect: nonreplacement pending rows become certified zero

`src/forecasting/ingestion.py:235–255` selects replacement rows before availability filtering, but drops future-processed delta/revision/counter rows at line 251. `snapshot.py:159–177` therefore never sees those pending rows, while its complete-day fill at lines 193–196 inserts a company zero. I executed a four-mode reproduction using one receipt available October 1, a September 1 row with value 100, `processed_at=October 2`, and complete/finalized coverage known October 1. Replacement produced `ready=False`, no amount and `processing_pending:billing:a`. Delta, revision and counter each produced **`ready=True`, company value 0, no limitations**. This is a real implementation gap, not merely a missing native connector.

Fix through the shared selection interface: expose known receipt/row metadata and pending states separately from usable numeric records. Snapshot must invalidate completeness for affected intervals while preserving a previously usable revision only under an explicit revision-pending policy. It must not read the withheld numeric value. Add all four modes, an earlier usable revision plus pending correction, out-of-order receipts, and serialized replay to regression coverage. Root was notified immediately.

## What already works

`Observation` (`ingestion.py:38`) preserves service date, source knowledge, ingestion, processing, typed statuses, Decimal values and financial basis. `available_at` takes their maximum. `SourceLedger.activate` distinguishes replacement/delta/revision/counter modes, rejects mode changes and incomplete generations, retains originals, handles A→B→A activation, and deduplicates overlapping economic records without requiring identical raw references. Counter reset epochs and cross-date counter ambiguity are guarded explicitly.

`SourceStore.acquire` (`source_store.py:50`) atomically persists source history and generation-bound coverage, isolates synthetic/real namespaces, and gives newly learned finality its true later knowledge time even when bytes repeat. `AnthropicCostReportAdapter` validates cursor chains, excludes unsupported billing scopes, preserves missing daily buckets as unknown, and does not treat a successfully fetched page as an invoice close. `build_snapshot` keeps measured partial amounts readable, rejects incompatible financial bases, checks current permissions and preserves company attribution totals. Existing 57 ingestion/source-store/provider/snapshot tests passed in 0.09 seconds in this audit. The counter pending defect was absent from those tests.

## Unified additions, in priority order

1. **Repair shared pending-observation selection**, as above. This protects backtests, training, apparent headroom and forecasts simultaneously.
2. **Add a small typed meter registry and observation envelope** adjacent to `Observation`, rather than new modules per provider. Required semantics: physical event/aggregate scope, exclusive component versus total/subset, cumulative versus increment, epoch, interval, requested/returned model and meter version, source-authoritative role, and known/partial/missing component mask. Provider input/cache and output/reasoning relationships are adapter-specific; preserve raw values and reject unsupported subtraction. Native telemetry parsing remains conditional on a verified adapter, but the normalized contract can support these semantics now.
3. **Make source reconciliation structured**: explicit covered accounts/products/intervals/measurement roles and matched/unmatched/overlap amounts. Current `source_partition_policy` is a nonempty assertion, and optional `economic_id` catches only supplied cross-source overlap. A proxy usage stream plus provider charge report cannot establish new expense by addition; preserve linked operational and financial views and unmatched amounts. Identity and financial-target agents own roster and target taxonomy details.
4. **Represent delay and observation policy as versioned state**: source reporting lag, collector instrumentation/sampling changes, suppression reason and historical receipt vintages. Preserve recorded recent incurred amounts separately from estimated missing incurred and genuine future usage. Existing service delayed-history bridge (`service.py:79–191`) already preserves those categories; no learned delay model exists. Estimate delay only from actual revisions, and expose sensitivity scenarios when usage-dependent missingness is unidentified. A collector heartbeat cannot establish complete enterprise routing.

## Scope and limits

Daily imported charge forecasts, replay and conservative missingness are in scope. Stream-to-daily normalization, source-specific delay nowcasts and representative sampling correction are conditional on event/receipt metadata; a daily total alone cannot reconstruct within-day increments or inclusion probabilities. Invoice/cash/general-ledger settlement remains outside this forecasting package; it should retain service-time evidence and hand off to the financial backend. Recursive autonomous budget enforcement is parked by the master plan. Model comparisons using public traces must state whether numbers are measured tokens, transformed list-price equivalents or real charges; public timestamps do not reconstruct historic acquisition availability. These limits narrow unsupported claims while preserving useful covered-source forecasts.
