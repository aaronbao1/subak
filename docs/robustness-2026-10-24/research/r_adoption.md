## Compact common mechanism

Add one bitemporal exposure/event contract beside accounting snapshots, not a separate feature per edge. `PersonSpell(person_id, spell_id, role/cohort, effective/known intervals)` links rehire spells without erasing prior use. `ExposureInterval` independently records employment, eligibility, provisioned access, at-risk time, and source-specific observation status. Use explicit unknowns; access and absence from logs are not latent engagement labels. `prior_use_status` is known-never, known-ever, or unknown; first-observed time is a different field from confirmed first-ever time.

Maintain predictive state `(engagement, elapsed_duration, ever_used, membership_spell)` with a small trial/active/dormant graph only when evidence supports those distinctions. A simpler pooled occurrence/count process remains a legitimate supported fallback. Known exposure gates constrain new human AI initiation; they do not cancel already-running workflows or manual opportunities. In full mode, one chronological opportunity/channel/admission/execution history determines first qualifying AI root and engagement updates. In reduced delivered-root mode, counts are already conditioned on execution; nonzero qualifying counts update first use, and are not filtered again. Keep this mode in the artifact so methods cannot be silently combined.

For a single constant competing-risk interval with trial hazard a, exit hazard b, duration d, the next trial probability is a/(a+b)*(1-exp(-(a+b)*d)); next exit is analogous and no event is exp(-(a+b)*d). This is an executable conservation check, not an end-of-day occupancy formula for reversible states. Repeated transitions require chronological simulation or a validated kernel. No use on one observed day does not imply dormancy, and latent state cannot be uniquely inferred from short all-zero histories.

## Highest priority implementation changes

1. Introduce exposure, prior-use knowledge and first-executed-root contracts; validate origin knowledge, spell boundaries and event uniqueness. Keep scalar accounting snapshots as a separate adapter.
2. Enforce first-trial/root conservation and declared full/reduced semantics in one execution/count kernel. Reject impossible transition/count combinations instead of repairing them with another gate.
3. Carry duration and uncertain initialization; use pooled effects and bounded rates, with shared rollout/policy shocks sampled once per path. Do not invent fitted personal coefficients from absent history.
4. Derive cumulative first adopters, current active users and starts from the same path with explicit denominators. Saturated first adoption can coexist with rising spend and reactivation. Keep permanent nonadoption and peer mechanisms conditional until follow-up/design supports them.
5. Run the edge tests in `edges/r_adoption.json` alongside temporal replay and mask sensitivity. They verify contracts/mechanics; held-out cohort calibration and final financial scores remain required for statistical promotion.

## Limits and discriminating tests

A public request trace without verified roster, eligibility, person identity or qualifying roots can test aggregate usage forecasts and provenance, but cannot validate adoption, retention or person forecasts. Proxy hashes must remain proxy identities. Short complete person usage can validate nonzero-use prediction, but cannot separate a cure fraction, latent dormancy, access friction and zero task need. For that data tier, fit the simpler pooled delivered process and present alternate rollout/access assumptions explicitly. A denied intent does not prove first trial; provisioning/login/test calls do not prove meaningful use; delayed events must not revise an earlier origin. Off-platform migration is missing observation for organization-wide usage but a real target exit for a narrowly defined provider charge target.

Falsifying scenarios include unknown initial prior use, rollout cancellation announced late, new eligible hires after apparent saturation, access loss with ongoing runs, and observation loss concentrated among dissatisfied users. Repeat the replay with post-origin evidence removed; results at that origin must be byte-stable. Agreement with a synthetic event graph establishes mechanism integrity only. State/cohort Brier/log scores, duration calibration and downstream priced horizon scores on real adequately observed cohorts are separate evidence.
