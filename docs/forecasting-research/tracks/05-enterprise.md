# 05 — Enterprise structured context: exposure, work mix, obligations, and identity

Research date: 2026-10-24. This is a fresh source-based investigation; no enterprise tenant was connected and no forecasting experiment was run. The documented APIs establish possible acquisition semantics, not the availability or predictive value of these fields at Northstar or another enterprise.

## Requirement and principal conclusion

The master plan's backend section requires explicit team mappings, source/period/coverage, distinguishable charges and estimates, versioned context, and persistent internal budgets. Its forecasting section leaves context use and granularity open. The mentor transcript, Speaker A at lines 52–54, proposes headcount as tractable; Speaker B at lines 43 and 49 proposes planned work and useful time windows. These are research hypotheses. Team membership is not necessarily exclusive, a seat is not activity, and activity is not automatically a charge. The customer is finance; employee-level joins are a modeling option, not a commitment to an individual performance interface.

**Inference:** Prioritize data that defines which spending and people are observable before collecting numerous contextual predictors. A rich current snapshot without historical knowledge times can make a retrospective model look better through leakage. Conversely, retaining unknown mappings can preserve reliable company totals while admitting weaker team coverage.

## Established evidence: what source systems actually expose

**Workforce.** BambooHR documents dated `jobInfo` rows for department, location, division, title, and manager, plus dated employment-status history. This provides an effective-date basis, but does not establish that every past edit is available. [BambooHR table fields](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/docs/table-name-fields). Its employee endpoint defaults to currently effective values; future-dated values require an option. Internal IDs differ from editable employee numbers. Unauthorized requested fields may be silently omitted, and record visibility depends on the integration principal. Therefore, HTTP success alone does not establish population or field coverage. [BambooHR Get Employee](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/get-employee).

The leave-request endpoint distinguishes requested, approved, denied, superseded, and cancelled states. An empty successful response can reflect insufficient employee visibility. Its update filter catches edits and state changes, but not permissions or related-name changes, and does not report requests that disappear. Pending-request edits can create a replacement ID. The source recommends an overlapping poll window. [BambooHR List Time Off Requests](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/list-time-off-requests). **Inference:** use leave intervals and status, not leave balances, as a potential exposure input; absence of returned leave is not certified availability.

**Project work.** Jira Cloud's dedicated changelog API is paginated, ordered oldest first, can filter fields, and respects project and issue permissions. Thus issue-state history is a distinct acquisition step from fetching current issues. [Jira Cloud REST v3 Issues](https://developer.atlassian.com/cloud/jira/platform/rest/v3/api-group-issues/#api-rest-api-3-changelog-bulkfetch-post). This establishes an interface for reconstructing changes, not proof that task labels, story points, or deadline proximity have a stable relationship with AI consumption. Jira custom-field definitions and the company's work taxonomy remain tenant-specific.

**Eligibility and adoption.** GitHub's seat API lists seats currently billed to an organization, with assignment timestamps and pending cancellation; it is documented as public preview. Its activity timestamp can depend on IDE telemetry. [Copilot user management API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/copilot/copilot-user-management). Usage reports are different: current documentation describes both client and server signals, uneven dimensional coverage, expected availability within two full UTC days, and organization reports beginning December 12, 2025. Organization activity follows membership and can appear in several organizations, while enterprise totals deduplicate. [Copilot usage concepts](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing-and-usage/copilot-usage-metrics/copilot-metrics). Per-user `ai_credits_used` is expressly consumption analysis, not an invoicing total; prompt, generation, and acceptance counters also measure different events. [Copilot usage fields](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/reference/copilot-usage-metrics/copilot-usage-metrics).

The daily user-team report excludes teams with fewer than five seated users, and members of multiple teams appear repeatedly. GitHub advises joining daily usage to same-day membership rather than joining a rolling report to today's members. Its team analytics deliberately let one user's activity contribute to several teams. [Team-level Copilot metrics](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/reference/copilot-usage-metrics/team-level-metrics). **Inference:** this hierarchy supports overlapping adoption views, not an additive finance allocation ledger. Suppression can mimic a team starting, stopping, or changing intensity.

**Procurement.** Coupa distinguishes PO header/lines, status, currency, update timestamps, internal revisions, and supplier-facing versions. A change need not advance the supplier version. Invoice-total visibility depends on API filter configuration. [Coupa Purchase Orders API](https://docs.coupa.com/en/developer-documentation/the-coupa-core-api/resources/transactional-resources/purchase-orders-api-purchase_orders). The current page was inspected through indexed primary documentation because direct opening redirected to a browser-support page; the legacy primary page independently showed these fields but warns it is unmaintained. **Inference:** PO amount alone cannot establish service dates, contract liability, invoice recognition, or cash timing. Those require the applicable contract/line facts and financial basis.

**Calendar.** Google Calendar distinguishes recurrence and exception identity, start/end time zones, attendee response, event type, and cancellation. Some cancelled records retain only identifiers; deleted entries eventually disappear. [Calendar event resource](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/events). Incremental sync includes deletions, requires completing pages, and can invalidate a token after expiry or ACL changes; recovery requires a fresh full sync. [Calendar synchronization](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/guides/sync). **Inference:** a calendar is a mutable statement of intended availability, not observed work or a permanent history archive.

**Identity.** SCIM specifies a provider-issued stable, non-reassignable resource ID and separately a client-issued external identifier. It does not make an email address a universal identity key or equate identities across providers. [RFC 7643 §3.1](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc7643/). The proposed identity model below extends this evidence; it is not supplied by SCIM itself.

## Proposed design: data value against acquisition burden

Ratings below are qualitative prioritization judgments, not measured forecast gains. Value depends on the target and tenant; burden includes historical reconstruction, permissions, semantic mapping, and ongoing reconciliation.

| Dataset | Potential value and candidate feature | Acquisition burden / principal ambiguity | Initial disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finance-approved team/account mapping and current allocation history | Defines accountable team targets; unmapped share; budget comparison | Low to medium for a curated import; organizational changes require ownership and dates | Required for additive team finance reporting; explicit unknown bucket |
| Worker status and department history | Eligible person-days; entrants/exits/transfers; change in team composition | Medium; effective dates are not first-known dates; contractors may be absent | Early if dated export exists; collect only required employment facts |
| Tool assignments, policy and rollout cohorts | Eligible seat-days; tenure since enablement; rollout phase | Medium; billed seats, authorized use and active users are different states | Early alongside provider usage, with separate state fields |
| Approved leave and working pattern | Planned available fraction by team/day | Medium to high; private access, cancellations, partial days, regional calendars | Optional experiment after headcount/eligibility baseline |
| Issue/project state and dated milestones | Work-type shares, approved starts, deadline distance, blocked/cancelled mix | High; workflow/custom-field mapping, project ownership, history completeness | Small curated project import before broad connector |
| Executed contracts, commitments, renewals, PO changes | Known fixed-fee/commitment schedule; remaining included entitlement | High semantic burden, but potentially direct value for a monetary target | Needed when target includes these charges; exclude unsupported categories explicitly |
| Internal budgets and decisions | Budget/forecast comparison and scenario assumptions | Low after backend integration; policy feedback and revised budgets can leak future decisions | Always available for workflow; predictor only in a separately evaluated variant |
| Public holidays and organization working calendar | Known future day-type exposure | Low to medium; individual region and overnight work can differ | Candidate basic covariate; do not presume weekends are zero |
| Personal calendar events | Planned absence/meeting load, deadlines corroboration | High; duplicates, recurrence, visibility and changing intent | Defer unless incremental value exceeds a coarser calendar |

A staffing export plus stable account mapping can be more useful initially than an automated HRIS connector with incomplete access. A curated milestone table can be more reproducible than extracting every task. Procurement moves earlier if finance requires total contractual charges; it can remain outside a usage-only target. Thus the ordering must be target-driven, not connector-count-driven.

## Proposed design: identity, responsibility, and point-in-time joins

Store source-scoped identities `(tenant, source, native_id)` separately from canonical entities. Represent human, service account, agent/run, project, organizational team, finance cost center, billing account and contract as different entity types. A cross-source identity assertion records matching method, evidence, reviewer or originating system, ambiguity, valid interval and knowledge interval. Email/login/name are time-bounded aliases; an email match alone must not silently merge two people after recycling or a rehire.

For each attributable usage event or charge retain independent roles:

- **Initiator:** human or upstream run that requested work.
- **Executor:** service principal, API key, tool account, or agent that performed it.
- **Beneficiary:** project/team receiving the work, possibly multiple or unknown.
- **Payer:** entity/account contractually billed, plus the separate internal allocation policy.

An engineering service account may execute research work requested by a product employee while a central account pays. Provider workspace, seat-assigning team, task assignee, manager and finance cost center are clues with different meanings. Preserve each rather than picking one as universal ownership. An explicitly approved allocation rule may distribute a charge by weights; overlapping membership analytics must remain labeled non-additive. For an additive view require `sum(team allocations) + unallocated = company amount` on the same currency, period and charge basis. A shared-account total may be forecastable even when individual attribution is unavailable. Do not fabricate equal splitting as an observation.

Use two temporal axes on facts and identity/membership assertions: business validity `[valid_from, valid_to)` and local knowledge `[known_from, known_to)`. Retain source timestamps, `first_observed_at`, ingestion completion, source revision/hash and any recorded publication timestamp separately. At forecast origin `o`, historical entity state for event time `t` must satisfy both `valid_from ≤ t < valid_to` and `known_from ≤ o < known_to`. Resolve conflicts explicitly; do not simply choose the most recently modified row across unrelated sources.

For a future date `d`, use only a plan version known by `o`, with its status and applicable interval; future validity is allowed, future knowledge is not. For example, a transfer announced on Monday for Friday may affect Friday exposure in Monday's forecast, but a correction first imported next Tuesday cannot enter Monday's replay. Aggregate daily exposure after these joins, then compute a 7/14/30-day or month-remainder target. Pin the output's ownership basis: organization as known at origin, actual historical membership, or a separately named restatement. Moving employees must not silently rewrite a prior finance forecast.

Historical source availability and local ingestion availability are different axes of evaluation. If a connector is first installed today, its historical records do not prove they would have been available to the forecasting system earlier. A counterfactual backtest can use independently demonstrated historical publication times, but must be labeled reconstructed and specify an availability-delay rule. Otherwise start evaluation after the first reliable snapshot. Store a connector capability manifest: requested/visible population and fields; scope/principal; source version; timezone; observation and ingestion windows; historic range; tombstone support; watermark; suppression rules; refresh delay; reconciliation status. Keep immutable evidence history separate from replaceable sync caches, so calendar resynchronization does not erase what an earlier forecast knew.

## Proposed design: feature eligibility and cold start

Define a feature gate per entity and origin, not merely per connector. A field can be `observed`, `planned`, `not_applicable`, `permission_limited`, `suppressed`, `late`, `unmapped`, or `unknown`. Null, zero, and no row are distinct. Gate individual features on identity resolution, relevant window coverage and correct event definitions; gate team features on adequate membership coverage and additive attribution when required. A missing project association need not invalidate the company's charge total.

Candidate exposure is a vector, not a universal scalar: employed person-days, approved available person-days, tool-enabled person-days, active-use person-days, and billed seat-days. A proposed identity such as `eligible exposure × usage intensity` is a modeling decomposition, not evidence that hires or absences cause proportional spend. Leave may reduce human initiation while jobs already launched continue; license cancellation may change eligibility before or after fees; work can transfer to colleagues.

A newly observed employee is not necessarily a new employee, and a new team can consist entirely of experienced users. Distinguish true entrant, internal transfer, return from leave, tool rollout, newly connected source, and newly resolved identity. Cold-start alternatives are a pooled model with tenure/cohort context, a team/role fallback, or a bounded scenario when evidence is inadequate. Transfer personal history only with a verified identity and allow changes in task/tool mix. New-hire counts without assigned tools support staffing scenarios, not a guaranteed spend increment. An announced hire without a firm start date should remain scenario evidence.

## Alternatives, falsification, and unresolved questions

**Recommended research sequence (inference):** establish historical spending and source coverage; add as-known membership and tool eligibility; then compare work-mix, leave/calendar, and procurement inputs appropriate to the target. Keep a spend-history-only baseline. Favor a small set of auditable covariates over broad ingestion until coverage is understood.

Evaluate with rolling forecast origins and identical eligible target sets. Compare (A) spending history, (B) history plus workforce/tool exposure, (C) B plus versioned projects, and (D) targeted leave/calendar features. Report error, interval calibration, unavailable rate, correction burden, and acquisition cost; stratify by true cold start, reorganization, rollout, suppression and missing-source conditions. Include a no-context fallback and delayed-source ablations. Do not reward a richer model for silently dropping difficult entities. A useful falsification is that B/C do not improve held-out performance or merely exploit final project states; then retain their explanatory role and remove numerical influence. A second falsification is that coarse team history outperforms individual exposure because automation persists independently of workforce changes.

**Unvalidated hypotheses:** work type and rollout tenure may predict intensity better than employee count; task counts may add little because teams ticket differently; approved leave may help short horizons but not automated workloads; budget may predict behavior only where teams respond to it. None has a supported numerical effect here. Correlation between budget and spend does not authorize enforcement or budget changes. The internal finance decision remains a reviewed backend action.

Unknowns requiring actual data include HRIS record completeness, contractor coverage, mapping error, historical plan snapshots, contract charging basis, provider metric retention by version, local holidays, and whether task ownership represents beneficiaries.
