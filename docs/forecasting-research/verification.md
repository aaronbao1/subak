## Evidence limits

Source verification used fresh primary-source browsing and selected versioned source-code inspection. Not every linked implementation was installed or executed; not every mutable source was archived. Some author/publisher pages were available only through abstracts or official indexed content, recorded in the source annotations. Licenses marked unverified remain unverified. Documentation proves documented capability, not tenant entitlement or complete historical data.

The 206 fault assertions remain proposed tests. Mathematical examples were checked as illustrations; they are not forecast experiments. The method taxonomy is broad within scope, not a formal exhaustive systematic review. Representative enterprise data, contract/account reconciliation, finance thresholds, live SDK credentials and prospective outcomes remain to be established. The next stage must report executed implementation evidence separately from these research checks.
